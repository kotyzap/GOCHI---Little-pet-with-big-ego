GOCHI

Play now · gochi.4xs.dev Get the box

A little pet with a very big ego. Feed it, flatter it, let it nap. It started on an ESP32 e-paper box. Now it also lives in your phone.

How it works

1

Sign up

A nickname and a 4-digit PIN. No email, nothing else.

2

Pick one

Eight gochis. Tap one and it's yours. No swapping and no reset until the game is over.

3

Look after it

Four bars drop in real time, also while you're away. Tap an action to push them back up.

4

Stay above zero

Any bar at 0 for 30 minutes and it's game over. Then you pick again.

Hint: the fastest possible game over takes about 30 minutes. Left alone, a gochi lasts 9 to 10 hours.

The cast

Two ways to play

Easy

In your phone

  • Open gochi.4xs.dev. Nothing to install.
  • Tap the screen: a gochi to choose it, an action to do it.
  • Same nickname and PIN on another phone = same gochi.
Play now
For makers

On the ESP32 box

  • Waveshare ESP32-S3-ePaper-1.54: 200 × 200 e-paper, battery, one button.
  • Short press = next, long press = do it.
  • Your gochi's energy can't go above the battery level. Charge the box and it recovers.
  • Build and flash it yourself from the source (Arduino).

Small print

21+ only. Gochi is for adults. By playing you confirm you are 21 or older.

Satire, for entertainment only. The gochis are cartoon parodies of the public personas of well-known politicians. Nothing here is a statement of fact, and no words or actions are attributed to any real person. Gochi is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to anyone it depicts.

No harm wanted. "Game over" means a game ended. It is not a wish, a threat or a call for harm to anyone. I joke about burgers, parades and long tables. I don't joke about health, age or family.

What I store. Your nickname, a hash of your PIN, and your gochi's game state. No email, no real name, no ads, no trackers. The sign-in is kept in your browser's storage.
